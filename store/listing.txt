=== Full description (max 4000) ===
Buzz into Doodle Bee, a world of 50 friendly puzzle games made for little hands and growing minds!

Doodle Bee helps children aged 2 to 6 practise counting, colours, shapes, letters, memory and problem solving, one happy level at a time. Every question is read aloud by a friendly voice, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

50 GAMES IN 5 WORLDS
• Jigsaw puzzles and sliding puzzles
• Counting, adding and taking away
• Spot the difference and hidden object hunts
• Colouring pages
• Mazes - help the dog, race the bee, collect the stars
• Trace numbers and letters
• Memory pairs and "follow the bee" sequences
• Sorting by colour, shape and shadow
• "What comes next?" patterns, odd one out, big and small
• Picture sudoku, connect the dots and spelling for older children

MADE FOR YOUNG CHILDREN
• Spoken instructions for every game - tap the speaker to hear them again
• Big, bright pictures and simple taps and drags
• Kind feedback: no game over, children can always keep trying
• Stars to collect, a new world to open, and a fireworks surprise at the end
• Gets gradually harder: the last world is fun up to age 8

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Up to 6 player profiles, each with its own picture and stars
• Music and voice can be switched on or off

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• Works completely offline - no internet needed
• No accounts, no chat, no tracking, no data collection
• Asks for no permissions
• Player names and progress stay on your device

Doodle Bee is made by Doodle Bees. We'd love to hear what your little ones think!
