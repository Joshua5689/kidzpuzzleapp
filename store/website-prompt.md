Build the website for **Little Hive Studio**, a small independent studio that makes gentle, safe games and learning apps for young children (ages 2–8). Our first app is **Doodle Bee – Puzzle and Play**; use it as the featured example throughout. The attached images are real artwork and screenshots from Doodle Bee: use them, and don't invent other app screenshots.

## Who it's for
Parents, grandparents, teachers and nursery staff deciding whether an app is good and safe for a small child. The tone is warm, calm, trustworthy and playful, never loud or salesy. Write in British English (colour, favourite, Mum).

## Look and feel
- A honey-and-hive theme: warm butter yellow (#F6D77A / #FFE9A8), cream backgrounds (#FFFAF0), deep navy text (#33334F), with soft accents of leaf green (#43B649), sky blue (#8ED3FF) and petal pink (#FF8FB8).
- Rounded, friendly typography: "Baloo 2" or "Fredoka" for headings, "Nunito" for body text (Google Fonts).
- Soft rounded cards (large radius), gentle shadows, a subtle honeycomb (hexagon) pattern used sparingly in backgrounds or section dividers, and small playful touches (a bee flying along a dotted path, light hover wiggle on cards). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Big, readable text and generous spacing. Fully responsive: looks great on phones (16px side padding, no sideways scrolling), tablets and desktop.
- Logo: the words "Little Hive Studio" with a small hexagon/bee mark. Make it a simple SVG.

## Technical
- A static marketing site: no database, no login, no backend, no cookies, no analytics or third-party trackers.
- Good SEO basics: page titles and meta descriptions, Open Graph image (use the banner), descriptive alt text on every image, semantic headings.
- Accessible: good colour contrast (navy text on cream/yellow), visible keyboard focus, buttons at least 44px tall.
- Keep the images optimised and lazy-loaded below the fold.
