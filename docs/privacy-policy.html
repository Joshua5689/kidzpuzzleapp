Doodle Bee – Puzzle and Play

Privacy Policy · Developer: Little Hive Studio · Effective date: 13 October 2026

In short: Doodle Bee does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The app works completely offline, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking, and asks for no device permissions.

Who we are

Doodle Bee – Puzzle and Play ("the app") is a puzzle game for young children made by Little Hive Studio (littlehivestudio.lovable.app). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the app keeps on the device

So that several children can share one device, a parent can create player profiles. A profile holds:

  • a name or nickname,
  • an age (2 to 8),
  • an optional gender (boy, girl or "skip"),
  • a picture chosen from the app's own cartoon animals,
  • game progress (which levels are finished and the stars earned) and settings (music and voice on or off).

This information is saved only in the app's private storage on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no internet connection. It is used only to show the player's name and picture in the game and to remember their progress.

You can delete a profile at any time in the app (Change player → Edit → Delete). Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Information we do not collect

  • No contact details, location, photos, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.
  • No accounts, logins, chat or social features.
  • No camera, microphone or other device permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children. Because it collects no personal information and sends nothing off the device, it is designed to meet the requirements of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and Google Play's Families Policy. We recommend that a parent or carer sets up the player profiles.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we never receive any.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in Google's own privacy policy. The app itself does not send any information to Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
